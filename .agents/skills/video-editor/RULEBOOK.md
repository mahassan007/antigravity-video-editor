# Editing rulebook

1. Start with the strongest clear opening that preserves the speaker's meaning.
2. Trim mistakes, repeated takes and distracting dead air. Keep meaningful pauses and full thoughts.
3. Preserve the real face and framing. Face retouching, replacement, reshaping and automatic punch-ins are off.
4. Keep the original voice. Avoid synthetic voiceovers and preserve meaning and delivery.
5. Use B-roll only when it shows or clarifies the spoken point. Keep the speaker's audio underneath.
6. Return to the speaker when the visual explanation ends. Keep the original shot when relevant B-roll is unavailable.
7. Make straight cuts the default. Shake, spin, bounce and decorative transitions are off.
8. Follow the brand sheet for captions and graphics. Keep text legible and clear of the face and platform controls.
9. Confirm names, numbers and technical terms against the brief and speech before rendering captions.
10. Leave music off by default. Use a supplied track only when the brief requests it.
11. Borrow observed pacing, hook structure and shot timing from the reference within these rules. Use supplied footage and graphics in the edit.
12. Check the final export for clipped speech, caption timing, B-roll sync, black frames and audio continuity before calling it complete.
